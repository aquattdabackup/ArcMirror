ArcMirror helps users understand USDC transactions on Arc Mainnet: where the money went, what gas cost, and why two logs can represent one payment.

This demo covers five transactions I created on mainnet:
• Native USDC transfer
• ERC-20 transfer and duplicate-log reconciliation
• Contract forwarding
• Batch payments
• An intentionally failed transaction

It also demonstrates Payout Reconciliation, Report Inspector, and Dust Lab.

ArcMirror is read-only and requires no wallet connection. Reports distinguish transaction success from evidence completeness. Dust Lab is an arithmetic simulation.
